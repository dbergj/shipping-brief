Freight rates

What ships earn per day
A freight rate is what someone pays to move cargo. The figures here are TCE — the ship's daily earnings after fuel and port costs — which is what actually decides whether an owner makes money. Greyed rows need a paid Baltic Exchange subscription, so they stay inactive; the labels are still here because knowing the vessel classes and routes matters either way.

Energy, gas and fuel

The costs and price gaps that drive shipping demand
Shipping demand is mostly about price gaps, not price levels. When a commodity is much dearer in one region than another, it gets shipped — and the further it travels, the more ships are needed.
